Neighborhood Profile

Downtown Brooklyn: the borough's hub.

Downtown Brooklyn is where Brooklyn does business. The area stretches from the Flatbush Avenue corridor to the civic center, anchored by the massive Atlantic Avenue-Barclays Center transit hub that channels nine subway lines and the LIRR into one of the busiest intersections in the outer boroughs.

Barclays Center at 620 Atlantic Avenue hosts over 200 events per year, Brooklyn Nets basketball, concerts, boxing, and cultural events that draw millions of visitors annually. Atlantic Terminal Mall and City Point (home to DeKalb Market Hall with 40+ food vendors) provide year-round retail and dining.

MetroTech Center houses NYU Tandon School of Engineering, JPMorgan Chase offices, and multiple city agencies. The Brooklyn Navy Yard to the north employs 10,000+ workers across its 300-acre innovation campus. Fulton Mall remains Brooklyn's busiest pedestrian shopping corridor.

FAQ

Downtown Brooklyn questions.

How far is Take N' Toke from Barclays Center?

Take N' Toke at 994 Atlantic Avenue is approximately 0.1 miles from Barclays Center, about a 2-minute walk east along Atlantic Avenue. Perfect for pre-event or post-event visits.

Can I visit before or after events at Barclays Center?

Absolutely. We're open during regular business hours and are one of the closest dispensaries to Barclays Center. We recommend visiting before events to avoid post-show crowds. Check our hours on the contact page.

Which subway lines bring me closest to the dispensary?

Atlantic Ave-Barclays Center station (2/3/4/5/B/Q/D/N/R) is just a 3-minute walk. Jay Street-MetroTech (A/C/F/R) is about 8 minutes on foot. The LIRR at Atlantic Terminal is also 3 minutes away.

Is there parking near the dispensary?

Multiple parking garages serve Downtown Brooklyn, including the Atlantic Terminal garage and lots along Flatbush Avenue Extension. Street parking is limited but available on some side streets.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash and debit cards. An ATM is available on-site. All prices include applicable taxes as required by New York State.
