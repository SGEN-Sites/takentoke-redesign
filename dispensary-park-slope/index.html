Neighborhood Profile

Park Slope: wellness meets community.

Park Slope is defined by Prospect Park, Frederick Law Olmsted's 526-acre masterpiece that forms the neighborhood's western boundary. Families, professionals, and long-time residents share tree-lined streets of Victorian brownstones and limestone rowhouses, creating one of Brooklyn's most desirable communities.

The Park Slope Food Coop, with over 16,000 members, reflects the neighborhood's values: quality, sustainability, and community ownership. Fifth Avenue and Seventh Avenue run parallel as the neighborhood's commercial spines, lined with independent bookstores, organic restaurants, wine bars, and family-owned businesses.

Grand Army Plaza anchors the northern edge with its monumental arch, the Saturday Greenmarket, and gateways to both Prospect Park and the Brooklyn Public Library. Park Slope residents are wellness-oriented, informed consumers who value transparency and quality. That is exactly what Take N' Toke delivers.

FAQ

Park Slope questions.

How far is Take N' Toke from central Park Slope?

We're at 994 Atlantic Avenue at the northern edge of Park Slope. From 5th Avenue and Union Street, it's about a 10-minute walk north. From the F/G at 7th Avenue, roughly 10-12 minutes. Atlantic Ave-Barclays Center is just 3 minutes from our door.

Do you carry wellness-oriented cannabis products?

Absolutely. Park Slope customers frequently ask about low-dose edibles, CBD-rich products, and balanced THC:CBD strains. Our staff can guide you toward products suited for relaxation, sleep, and general wellness without overwhelming psychoactive effects.

Can I stop by after the Saturday Greenmarket at Grand Army Plaza?

Yes. Grand Army Plaza is about a 12-minute walk southeast. Many customers combine their Saturday Greenmarket trip with a dispensary visit. We're open during Greenmarket hours.

Is there parking available for Park Slope customers?

Street parking is available on Atlantic Avenue and surrounding side streets with metered spots. There's also a parking garage near Barclays Center, one block west. Many Park Slope customers walk or bike.

Do I need to be a New York resident to purchase?

No. Any adult 21 or older with a valid government-issued photo ID can purchase. New York residents and visitors are both welcome.
