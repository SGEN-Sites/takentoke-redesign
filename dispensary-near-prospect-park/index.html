Park-Adjacent

Minutes from the Long Meadow.

Prospect Park is Brooklyn's 526-acre backyard, designed by Frederick Law Olmsted and Calvert Vaux, the same pair behind Central Park, and many locals consider it their finest work. Its 3.35-mile loop draws runners and cyclists at dawn, the rolling Long Meadow fills with picnic blankets by afternoon, and the LeFrak Center and Prospect Park Zoo keep families busy year-round.

Take N' Toke sits just north of all of it. From our door at 994 Atlantic Avenue in Prospect Heights, the park's grand Grand Army Plaza entrance is about an 8-minute walk south, roughly six blocks down Washington or Underhill Avenue. Along the way you pass the Brooklyn Public Library, and at the park's north end sit the Brooklyn Botanic Garden and the Saturday Greenmarket at Grand Army Plaza.

That makes us a natural first stop for anyone planning a park day. Browse lab-tested flower, precisely dosed edibles, grab-and-go pre-rolls, and discreet vapes from New York-licensed cultivators, set them aside for later, then head down to the park. We are open daily until 10:00 PM, walk-in only, no appointment, no online ordering, no delivery. Just bring a valid government photo ID showing you are 21 or older.

FAQ

Prospect Park questions.

Is there a dispensary near Prospect Park?

Yes. Take N' Toke is a licensed adult-use dispensary at 994 Atlantic Avenue, about an 8-minute walk north of the Grand Army Plaza entrance to Prospect Park. Head south on Washington or Underhill Avenue, roughly six blocks, and you reach the park's northern gateway.

How far is Take N' Toke from Prospect Park?

Roughly six blocks, about an 8-minute walk. From our door at 994 Atlantic Avenue, walk south on Washington Avenue or Underhill Avenue and you arrive at Eastern Parkway and Grand Army Plaza, the northern entrance to the park.

Can I walk to Prospect Park from the dispensary?

Yes, easily. It is a flat, straightforward walk south through Prospect Heights. Stop at the shop first, then continue down Washington or Underhill toward Grand Army Plaza and the Long Meadow. The whole route takes about 8 minutes on foot.

Can I consume cannabis in Prospect Park?

No. Smoking, vaping, and consuming cannabis are not permitted in New York City parks, including Prospect Park, and public consumption is restricted citywide. Pick up what you like for later and enjoy it at home or another private space where consumption is allowed.

What are your hours for a park day?

We are open daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, so you can stop in on your way to a morning run, an afternoon picnic, or an evening stroll. We are walk-in only, no online ordering and no delivery.

What should I bring?

Bring a valid government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older, it is required for every visit. We accept cash and debit. For a park day, plan ahead: get your shopping done first, since consumption is not allowed in the park itself.
