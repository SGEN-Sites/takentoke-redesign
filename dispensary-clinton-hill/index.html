Neighborhood Profile

Clinton Hill: brownstone Brooklyn.

Clinton Hill is one of Brooklyn's most architecturally rich neighborhoods, defined by landmarked 19th-century brownstones and tree-lined streets. The neighborhood attracts young professionals, grad students from Pratt Institute, and creative workers who value its residential calm and proximity to Downtown Brooklyn and Fort Greene.

Myrtle Avenue serves as Clinton Hill's commercial spine, with independent restaurants, coffee shops, and boutiques that give the area its distinct neighborhood feel. The Wallabout area to the north connects Clinton Hill to the Brooklyn Navy Yard and its growing creative economy.

With The Dekalb, Brooklyn's tallest tower, reshaping the western skyline and new development along Atlantic Avenue, Clinton Hill is evolving while maintaining the residential character that makes it one of Brooklyn's most desirable neighborhoods. Take N' Toke is an easy walk from anywhere in the area.

FAQ

Clinton Hill questions.

How close is Take N' Toke to Clinton Hill?

We're at 994 Atlantic Avenue, right at the southern boundary of Clinton Hill. From the G train at Clinton-Washington Aves, it's about a 5-7 minute walk. From Myrtle Avenue, roughly 8-10 minutes south.

Which subway lines connect Clinton Hill to the dispensary?

The G train at Clinton-Washington Aves is closest at 5-7 minutes. The C train at Clinton-Washington is 8-10 minutes. Atlantic Terminal (2/3/4/5) and Barclays Center (B/Q/D/N/R) are just 3 minutes from our door.

Do I need an appointment to visit?

No appointment needed. Take N' Toke is walk-in only. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID proving you're 21 or older.

What types of products are popular with Clinton Hill locals?

Clinton Hill's young professionals and grad students tend to favor our vape cartridges for discretion and convenience, along with low-dose edibles. Our flower selection also moves quickly in this neighborhood. Staff can guide you to the right product.

Is there bike parking near the store?

Yes. Bike racks are available along Atlantic Avenue and surrounding streets. Clinton Hill is one of Brooklyn's most bike-friendly neighborhoods, with protected lanes on several nearby streets.
