Cannabis Pre-Rolls in Brooklyn

Pre-rolls are ready-to-smoke joints, rolled with lab-tested flower and sealed for freshness. No grinding, no rolling, no equipment. Just light it and go. The most convenient way to enjoy cannabis flower.

Fundamentals

What are pre-rolls?

Pre-rolls are joints that come ready to smoke. Ground cannabis flower is packed into a rolling paper with a filter tip, sealed, and packaged in an airtight tube. They're produced by licensed processors using the same lab-tested flower sold on dispensary shelves.

For beginners, pre-rolls eliminate the learning curve of grinding and rolling. For regular consumers, they offer consistency and convenience. For social settings, they're the easiest format to share.

Effects mirror flower since that's what they are: rapid onset (1-5 minutes), 1-3 hour duration, and the full terpene and cannabinoid profile of the strain inside. Infused pre-rolls run hotter, hitting harder and lasting longer due to added concentrate.

Pre-Roll Types

Singles, packs, and infused.

Pre-rolls come in several formats to match different situations, tolerance levels, and preferences.

Singles

0.5-1g

One joint, one session. The most straightforward option. Perfect for trying a new strain without committing to a full pack. Available in every strain we carry.

Best for

Solo sessions, sampling new strains, on-the-go convenience.

Multi-Packs

3-5 joints

Multiple pre-rolls packaged together, typically 0.5g each. Better per-joint value than singles. Great for sharing or stocking up on a strain you already know you like.

Best for

Social gatherings, weekend supply, sharing with friends.

Infused

0.5-1g + concentrate

Standard flower rolled with concentrate (distillate, live resin, or kief) for elevated potency. Significantly stronger than regular pre-rolls. Not recommended for beginners.

Best for

Experienced consumers seeking higher potency, special occasions.

King-Size

1.5-2g

Oversized joints for extended sessions or group settings. More flower per roll means longer burn time and a fuller experience. A statement piece for those who like to share.

Best for

Group sessions, parties, extended personal sessions.

Strain Selection

Choosing by time of day.

The strain inside the pre-roll determines the experience. Here is a simple framework for matching your pre-roll to the moment.

Energizing

Daytime (Sativa-Leaning)

Uplifting, energetic, cerebral effects. Choose sativa or sativa-dominant hybrid pre-rolls for creative projects, social outings, walks through Prospect Park, or anything that benefits from an active, engaged mindset.

Relaxing

Evening (Indica-Leaning)

Relaxing, sedating, body-heavy effects. Indica or indica-dominant hybrid pre-rolls are ideal for winding down after work, movie nights, sleep support, or low-key evenings at home.

Versatile

Anytime (Balanced Hybrid)

A middle ground that blends gentle energy with light relaxation. Balanced hybrids work for social settings, casual afternoons, or when you want versatility without committing to one direction.

Quality Check

What makes a good pre-roll.

Not all pre-rolls are created equal. Here is what separates a quality joint from a mediocre one.

Rolling Quality

A well-rolled joint is firm but not too tight, with an even density from tip to filter. It should feel solid when gently squeezed but not rock-hard. Loose rolls canoe and burn unevenly.

Flower Quality

The best pre-rolls use the same quality flower sold on the shelf, not shake or trim. Check the label: reputable brands specify whole-flower or note the source material. Ask our staff about what's inside.

Burn Characteristics

A quality pre-roll burns evenly with white or light gray ash. Dark ash, canoeing (one side burning faster), or frequent relighting suggests poor construction or improperly cured flower.

Potency Info

All pre-rolls carry lab-tested THC/CBD percentages on the label. Infused joints will show significantly higher THC. Always check the label before lighting, especially with infused products.

Paper Types

What it's wrapped in.

The rolling paper affects burn rate, flavor, and overall experience. Most pre-rolls at Take N' Toke use natural, unbleached papers.

01

Natural (Unbleached)

Standard rolling papers made from hemp, rice, or unbleached wood pulp. Thin, minimal flavor interference, and even burn. The most common and recommended option.

02

Hemp Wraps

Made from hemp fiber, offering a slightly thicker roll with a subtle earthy flavor. Burns slower than standard papers. A natural complement to cannabis flower.

03

Blunt Wraps

Thicker wraps made from tobacco leaf or tobacco-free alternatives. Slower burn, fuller body, and a distinctive experience. Tobacco-free options available for those who prefer to avoid nicotine entirely.

Storage Tips

Keep them fresh.

Pre-rolls are best consumed fresh. Proper storage preserves the flavor, burn quality, and potency of the flower inside.

01Keep pre-rolls in their original sealed tube or container.

02Store at room temperature away from direct sunlight and heat.

03Avoid bending, crushing, or storing loosely in pockets.

04Consume within 2-4 weeks for optimal freshness and flavor.

05If a pre-roll dries out, it will burn faster and harsher. Proper storage prevents this.
