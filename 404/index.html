21+ OnlyNYS OCM Cannabis Warning

For use only by adults 21 years of age and older. Keep out of reach of children and pets. In case of accidental ingestion or overconsumption, contact the Poison Center hotline 1-800-222-1222 or call 9-1-1. Please consume responsibly. Cannabis is not recommended for use by persons who are pregnant or nursing. Concerned about your cannabis use? Contact the New York State HOPELine by texting “HopeNY,” calling 1-877-8-HOPENY, or visiting oasas.ny.gov/HOPELine.
