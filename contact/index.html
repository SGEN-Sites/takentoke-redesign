For use only by adults 21 years of age and older. Keep out of reach of children and pets. Cannabis is not recommended for use by persons who are pregnant or nursing. Concerned about your cannabis use? Contact the New York State HOPELine by texting “HopeNY,” calling 1-877-8-HOPENY, or visiting oasas.ny.gov/HOPELine.
