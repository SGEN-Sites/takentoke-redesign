Arena-Adjacent

The closest walk-in dispensary to the arena.

Barclays Center draws millions of fans a year for Nets basketball, marquee concerts, and championship boxing, and it sits right on Atlantic Avenue, the same corridor as Take N' Toke. From the arena, our door at 994 Atlantic Avenue is a straight, flat 12-minute walk east.

That makes us a natural pre-show stop for anyone heading to an event: pick up grab-and-go pre-rolls, precisely dosed edibles, or a discreet vape from New York-licensed cultivators, then make your way back to the arena. Everything we carry is lab-tested and sold by knowledgeable budtenders who can point you to the right product fast.

We are open daily until 10:00 PM, walk-in only, no appointment, no online ordering, no delivery. Just bring a valid government photo ID showing you are 21 or older.

FAQ

Barclays Center questions.

Is there a cannabis dispensary near Barclays Center?

Yes. Take N' Toke is a licensed adult-use dispensary at 994 Atlantic Avenue, about a 12-minute (0.6 mile) walk east on Atlantic Avenue from Barclays Center. It is one of the closest licensed, walk-in dispensaries to the arena.

How far is Take N' Toke from Barclays Center?

Roughly 0.6 miles, about a 12-minute straight walk east along Atlantic Avenue, or two minutes on any train to the Atlantic Ave-Barclays Center hub plus the short walk.

Can I stop by before a Nets game or concert?

Absolutely. We are open daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, so you can stop in before most events. We recommend arriving early on big event nights, bring a valid government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older.

Do I need to order ahead?

No. Take N' Toke is walk-in only, there is no online ordering or delivery. Browse our live menu online to plan your visit, then shop in person with help from our budtenders.

Are you open after events let out?

We close at 10:00 PM daily. For later evening shows, plan to visit before the event rather than after. Check our live hours on the location page before you head over.
