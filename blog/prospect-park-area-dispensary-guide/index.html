Finding a Licensed Dispensary Near Prospect Park

Prospect Park sits at the center of some of Brooklyn's best neighborhoods. Whether you are coming from a morning run, an afternoon at the botanic garden, or a weekend picnic, here is how to reach a licensed dispensary from the park and surrounding areas.

Shop NowMore Guides

Neighborhoods

Prospect Park Neighborhoods

01

5-15 min walk to TNT

Prospect Heights

North side of the park

The closest Prospect Park neighborhood to Take N' Toke. Walk south on Vanderbilt or Washington Avenue and you are on Atlantic in minutes. Prospect Heights residents can reach 994 Atlantic Avenue faster than most people reach their subway station.

2/3 at Eastern Parkway, B/Q at Atlantic Ave-Barclays · 02 · 15-25 min walk

Park Slope

South and west of the park

Park Slope sits on the western side of Prospect Park. From the north end of the Slope near Flatbush Avenue, you can walk to Atlantic Avenue in about 15 minutes. From deeper in the Slope, consider the B/Q or F/G trains to get north faster.

F/G at 7th Ave or 4th Ave-9th St, B/Q at Atlantic · 03 · 20-30 min walk

Windsor Terrace

Southeast of the park

Windsor Terrace is a quieter neighborhood on the park's southeastern edge. The walk to Atlantic Avenue is longer, but the F/G train from Fort Hamilton Parkway connects you to Downtown Brooklyn in about 10 minutes, putting you close to TNT.

F/G at Fort Hamilton Pkwy, B68 bus on Prospect Park SW

On Foot

Walking from the Park to Atlantic Avenue

The most direct route from Prospect Park to Take N' Toke is to exit the park on the north side, near Grand Army Plaza or along Flatbush Avenue, and walk north on any of the avenues that cross Atlantic: Vanderbilt, Washington, Classon, or Franklin.

From the Grand Army Plaza entrance, it is about a 10-minute walk north to 994 Atlantic Avenue. The route takes you through the heart of Prospect Heights, past restaurants, cafes, and neighborhood shops. It is a pleasant walk in any season.

If you are on the south or west side of the park, cutting through to the north exits is faster than walking around the perimeter. The park's internal paths are well-marked and bring you to Grand Army Plaza in about 15 to 20 minutes from most southern entrances.

Culture

Post-Park Shopping and Cannabis Culture

Combining a park visit with a dispensary stop has become part of the Brooklyn routine for many locals. The area north of Prospect Park is full of places to eat, drink, and shop. A dispensary visit fits naturally into an afternoon that includes the park, the Brooklyn Museum, or the botanic garden.

Cannabis culture around Prospect Park is relaxed and community-oriented. This is a neighborhood where people know their budtenders by name and where a dispensary visit is as normal as a coffee run. The goal is not to make cannabis special. It is to make it accessible.
