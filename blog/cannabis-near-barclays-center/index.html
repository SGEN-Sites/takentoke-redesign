Cannabis Near Barclays Center

Heading to a Nets game, a concert, or a show at Barclays Center? Take N' Toke is the closest licensed walk-in dispensary, about a 12-minute walk east on Atlantic Avenue. Here is how to plan your stop and do it right.

Directions from BarclaysBrowse the Menu

The Venue

A Quick Word on Barclays Center

Barclays Center sits at 620 Atlantic Avenue, on the corner of Atlantic and Flatbush, and has anchored this stretch of Brooklyn since it opened in 2012. It is home to the Brooklyn Nets and the New York Liberty, and it hosts concerts, championship boxing, college basketball, and family shows throughout the year.

That puts the arena on the same avenue as Take N' Toke. From the Barclays exit, our door at 994 Atlantic Avenue is a straight, flat walk east, no transfers, no detours. For event dates and tickets, check the official calendar at barclayscenter.com; we are a dispensary down the street, not a ticket seller.

The Walk

The Closest Licensed Walk-In Dispensary

Take N' Toke is about 0.6 miles, roughly a 12-minute walk, east of Barclays Center along Atlantic Avenue. We are a licensed New York adult-use dispensary, open daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, walk-in only. Everything we carry is lab-tested and sourced from New York-licensed cultivators.

For a step-by-step walking route from the arena, transit details, and what regulars pick up, see our dispensary near Barclays Center page.

Plan Ahead

How to Plan Your Pre-Event Stop

  • 1. Go before, not after. We close at 10:00 PM. For most evening events, plan to stop in on your way to the arena.
  • 2. Browse the menu first. Check our live menu online so you know what you want before you walk in, it keeps the visit quick on a busy night.
  • 3. Bring your ID. A valid government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older is required for entry. No medical card needed.
  • 4. Give yourself time. Event nights bring crowds to the whole Atlantic Avenue corridor. Build in a few extra minutes.

Do It Right

Know Before You Go

A few things to keep in mind so your night goes smoothly. You cannot bring cannabis into Barclays Center, and you cannot consume cannabis in the arena or in public, that includes sidewalks, streets, and transit. Keep any purchase sealed for later use at a private residence.

Never drive after consuming, and never consume before driving. If you are heading home after an event, the Atlantic Ave-Barclays Center hub puts nine subway lines and the LIRR right there. Always check Barclays Center's current bag and prohibited-items policy before you arrive.

FAQ

Barclays Center Cannabis FAQ

Is there a dispensary near Barclays Center?

Yes. Take N' Toke is a licensed adult-use dispensary at 994 Atlantic Avenue, about a 12-minute (0.6 mile) walk east on Atlantic Avenue from Barclays Center. It is one of the closest licensed, walk-in dispensaries to the arena.

Can I bring cannabis into Barclays Center?

No. Like most arenas, Barclays Center prohibits bringing cannabis inside, and you cannot consume cannabis in the venue or in public. Always check the venue's current bag and prohibited-items policy before you go, and keep any purchase sealed for later use at a private residence.

How far is Take N' Toke from Barclays Center?

About 0.6 miles, roughly a 12-minute straight walk east along Atlantic Avenue, or a couple of minutes on any train to the Atlantic Ave-Barclays Center hub plus a short walk.

What time should I go before an event?

Take N' Toke is open daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, so plan to stop in before your event rather than after. Arrive with extra time on busy event nights, and bring a valid government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older. We are walk-in only.

Where can I see what's happening at Barclays Center?

Check the official Barclays Center event calendar at barclayscenter.com for the current schedule of Nets and New York Liberty games, concerts, boxing, and family shows. We do not sell event tickets, we are a cannabis dispensary down the street.
