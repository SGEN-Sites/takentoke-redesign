Licensed Retail

Why Licensed Matters

Take N' Toke is licensed by the New York Office of Cannabis Management for adult-use retail sales. We operate fully within state law, which means every product is lab-tested, tracked from seed to sale, and sold only to adults 21 and older with valid identification.

Unlike unlicensed smoke shops, our products carry verified test results. You know the potency, the ingredients, and that it has been checked for contaminants. That matters.

We pay state and local taxes, follow OCM packaging and labeling rules, and keep our license current. When you buy from Take N' Toke, you support the legal market and get a safer product.

First Visit

Tips for your first visit.

Never been to a dispensary? Here is what to know before you walk in.

Bring Valid ID

You must be 21 or older. We accept any government-issued photo ID: driver's license, passport, or state ID card.

Know Your Budget

Products range from $15 to $80+. Pre-rolls and small edibles start low. Our staff can recommend products at any price point.

Ask Questions

We explain everything without judgment. THC percentages, terpenes, consumption methods, expected effects. Just ask.

Cash and Card

We accept debit cards and cash. ATM available on-site. No credit cards due to federal banking regulations.

FAQ

Brooklyn dispensary questions

What Brooklyn neighborhoods does Take N' Toke serve?

Take N' Toke is a licensed adult-use dispensary at 994 Atlantic Avenue, on the Prospect Heights/Clinton Hill border. We're a short trip for Crown Heights, Fort Greene, Park Slope, Bed-Stuy, Boerum Hill, Downtown Brooklyn, and the Atlantic Avenue-Barclays Center area.

Where is the closest licensed dispensary to me in Brooklyn?

If you're near Prospect Heights, Crown Heights, Fort Greene, or Clinton Hill, Take N' Toke at 994 Atlantic Avenue is one of the closest licensed options. Browse our live menu online, then shop in person.

Is Take N' Toke a licensed dispensary?

Yes. Take N' Toke is licensed by the New York State Office of Cannabis Management (OCM). Our license is posted in store, and you can verify it in the OCM Dispensary Location Verification tool.

What are Take N' Toke's hours?

We're open daily, 10AM to 10PM, at 994 Atlantic Avenue, Brooklyn, NY 11238. Walk in any day of the week.

Do I need an appointment, or can I just walk in?

No appointment needed, Take N' Toke is walk-in only. Bring a valid 21+ government-issued ID and shop in person with help from our budtenders.

What do I need to bring to the dispensary?

A valid government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older. That's required to enter and purchase. No medical card is needed for adult-use cannabis.
